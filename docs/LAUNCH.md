# Meme-ology launch copy

## Short post

Meme-ology is a source-aware field guide to internet culture.

Search Know Your Meme entries, open a permanent source-linked page, inspect the dates and record IDs, and copy the same response through a small FastAPI playground.

Get the joke. Find the source.

## Longer post

I rebuilt Meme-ology around a simple rule: a meme record should show where it came from and what the source actually proves.

The new explorer has a warm editorial reading experience, direct entry URLs, uncropped examples, source and retrieval dates, a reproducible API playground, and explicit offline or unavailable states. The Know Your Meme catalog is kept separate from observed source popularity so a documented entry is not dressed up as a live trend.

The launch stays deliberately narrow. Instagram and TikTok trend detection will need permitted source access and a measured history before the product can make that claim.
